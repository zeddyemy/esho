Admin guide

Everything on the website that changes often can be edited from the admin at /admin. Changes appear on the live site within about a minute.

Signing in

  1. Go to /admin on your website and sign in with your email and password.
  2. Forgot it? Choose Forgot password. A reset link is emailed to you. It opens a page where you choose a new password.
  3. To change your password while signed in: Settings, then Password at the bottom.

Passwords need at least 10 characters with a letter and a number. Nobody else can create an account: sign-ups are switched off.

Projects

  1. Projects in the sidebar lists everything, in the order shown on the Work page. Drag the handle to reorder.
  2. The switch on each row publishes or hides a project. The star features it in Selected work on the homepage.
  3. New project asks for a title, then opens the editor. New projects are drafts until you switch on Published.
  4. In the editor: cover image (required to publish), summary, role, year, tags (they power the filter on the Work page), tools, outcomes, links, gallery and the case study write-up.
  5. Press Save project in the bar at the bottom. Leaving with unsaved changes asks you first.

Writing

  1. Writing lists articles. Search, sort and filter between published and drafts.
  2. New article, then write in the editor. Use Preview to see it exactly as it will look.
  3. Switch on Published and save. The publish date defaults to today. Pick a future date to schedule it.
  4. Reading time is worked out for you.

To hide the whole Writing section (page, menu link, homepage block and RSS feed), use Settings, On the website.

Page text

Page content has a tab per page. Each block saves on its own.

TabWhat you can change
HomepageHeadline and buttons, the introduction statement, the capabilities grid (a capability without a photo becomes a dark highlight block), the closing call to action.
AboutHeading, introduction, photo, your story, quick facts and working principles.
ContactHeading, text, response-time note, and the choices in the form's "What do you need?" and "Budget" lists.
Work, WritingThe heading and introduction at the top of those pages.

Experience, stack and testimonials

Experience holds work and education entries. Drag to order them (most recent first). The homepage shows the top three work roles. Leave Ended empty for a current role.

Stack holds the tool groups on the About page. Type a tool and press Enter to add it.

Testimonials are the quotes on the homepage. Keep them short.

Settings and résumé

  • Identity: brand (the big wordmark), your name, role, portrait and optional logo.
  • Availability: the line at the top of the homepage, and whether you show as open to work.
  • Contact: email, phone numbers and WhatsApp. Adding a WhatsApp number shows the floating chat button.
  • Social links: leave a field empty to hide that link.
  • Résumé: upload a PDF (up to 10 MB). The link /resume always opens the latest one, so you can share it anywhere.
  • On the website: switch Writing, Testimonials and the résumé download on or off.
  • Search and sharing: the homepage title, description and preview image used by Google and link previews.

Enquiries

Messages from the contact form arrive in Enquiries. The sidebar shows a count of new ones. Opening one marks it as read. Reply by email opens your mail app with a draft and marks it as replied. Archive anything you are done with.

Images

  • JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC. Large photos are resized automatically.
  • Add a short description to every image. It helps search engines and screen readers.
  • Replaced or removed images are deleted from storage when you save.
